01 / About me

Thoughtful analysis.
Useful outcomes.

I combine structured data work with visual storytelling, keeping the business question at the centre of every analysis.

A browser-first approach to data.

I am a Data Analyst focused on a browser-based workflow. I perform end-to-end data analysis from data preparation and cleaning to analysis and visualization — using tools like Kaggle, notebooks, Google Colab, and Google Sheets. This allows me to deliver accurate insights without being limited by hardware and to verify all results before delivery.

My approach is practical: understand the data, check its quality, investigate patterns, and communicate findings in a way that is easy to understand and act on.
